# ACTIFY Demo Flow

1. Open ACTIFY on the iQOO phone.
2. Capture a real assignment/notice with the camera.
3. Extract the text and send it to the AI understanding pipeline.
4. Show the structured task, deadline and priority.
5. Add the task to the schedule.
6. Show the **Next Best Action**.
7. Introduce a schedule conflict such as a meeting.
8. Replan and show the updated schedule with an explanation.
9. Optionally repeat the flow with voice or screenshot input.
